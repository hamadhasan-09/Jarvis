# Research Skill

## Purpose
Perform evidence-driven research rather than relying on unsupported model memory.

## Workflow
1. Identify the exact research question.
2. Determine whether current information is required.
3. Retrieve authoritative and relevant sources.
4. Cross-check important claims.
5. Separate facts from interpretation.
6. Note uncertainty, conflicts, and source limitations.
7. Synthesize the evidence into a useful answer.
8. Cite claims that depend on retrieved sources.

## Rules
- Do not fabricate sources.
- Do not claim live information without live retrieval.
- Prefer primary sources for technical, legal, financial, and official information.
- Treat search snippets as discovery material, not final evidence.
